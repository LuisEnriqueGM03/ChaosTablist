✦ Chaos Tablist ✦

Customize your tab list with animations, gradients, pixel-art icons and live stats, all edited in-game with a live preview.

✨ Features

  • Animated header and footer with several lines: gradients, rainbow, fade, wave, typewriter, marquee and more.
  • 162 pixel-art icons and badges, plus your own PNG images.
  • Live data: TPS, RAM, CPU, ping, players online, world, coordinates, FPS, local time and 80+ placeholders.
  • Chaos Ranks support: each player shows their rank badge next to their name.
  • Works with vanilla clients. Players who also have the mod get smooth 60 FPS animations, text backgrounds, glow, player heads and a styled panel.

🎨 In-game editor

Type /tablist to open the editor with a live preview. Changes apply to the whole server as soon as you save.

  • Click ✎ on any effect to set its colors, speed, and text or icon.
  • Try a color in the preview before you accept it.
  • Double-click a line to edit it in place. Ctrl+Z undoes and Ctrl+Y redoes.

📋 Commands (OP)

  • /tablist: open the editor
  • /tablist reload: reload the config
  • /tablist toggle: turn the custom tab on or off
  • /tablist player <players> ...: give a player their own format, or hide them

Requires: Fabric API · Minecraft 1.21.1 · Languages: English and Spanish
